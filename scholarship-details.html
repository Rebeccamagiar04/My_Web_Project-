DAAD Scholarship

Master's / PhD
Country Germany
Study Level Master's / PhD
Funding Fully Funded

About

The DAAD (German Academic Exchange Service) offers postgraduate scholarships to graduates and academics from developing and industrialised nations to pursue degree programs or research at state-recognized German universities.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Must hold at least a Bachelor's degree or equivalent in a relevant discipline completed within the last 6 years.
  • Candidates for EPOS courses generally require at least 2 years of professional work experience after graduation.
  • Highly qualified graduates with strong academic track records committed to local development.
  • Your most recent university degree should generally not be older than 6 years at the time of application.
  • You must be a citizen of a country eligible for DAAD funding.
  • Must not have lived in Germany for longer than 15 months at the time of application.
  • For German-taught programs, you need proof of German proficiency (TestDaF, Goethe Certificate B2/C1) and standard academic references/recommendation letters.

Essential Documentation Checklist

To submit a complete DAAD application on the portal, applicants must prepare:

  • Curriculum Vitae (CV): Tabular format (typically Europass style), reverse chronological, with no unexplained gaps.
  • Letter of Motivation: 1 to 3 pages explaining academic and career reasons for choosing Germany and the specific study program.
  • Academic Transcripts & Certificates: Bachelor's degree certificate and complete transcript showing individual grades and grading scale.
  • Letter of Recommendation: From a university lecturer or supervisor on official letterhead.
  • Language Certificates: IELTS, TOEFL, or TestDaF/Goethe as required by the chosen degree.
  • University Admission Letter: (If already received; otherwise, proof of university application can be submitted, and admission uploaded prior to award finalization).

What It Covers

  • Monthly Stipend: €934 to €992 for Master's candidates and €1,300 to €1,400 for Doctoral candidates to cover housing, food, local transport, and personal day-to-day expenses.
  • Health, Accident, & Personal Liability Insurance Coverage: Pays directly toward your comprehensive health and liability insurance policies required in Germany.
  • Travel Allowance: Covers international flight tickets to and from Germany.
  • Annual Study & Research Allowance: Typically €460 to assist with books, study materials, or printing costs, along with language course subsidies.
  • Tuition Fees Coverage: Public universities in Germany do not charge tuition fees; however, for specific modules/courses that do charge tuition, DAAD often covers or waives these fees.
  • Semester & Transit Contributions: Covers local public transit tickets and administrative campus fees.
  • Language Course: Free intensive German language course (usually 2 to 6 months in Germany prior to the start of your degree).
  • Family Allowance: Extra monthly stipend if you are accompanied by a spouse and/or children.
  • Disability / Chronic Illness Support: Financial assistance for justified costs related to accessibility or medical care.

Application Deadline

Deadlines vary between August and November annually depending on the university course selected.

UNHCR DAFI Scholarship

Undergraduate
Country Various (Country of Asylum)
Study Level Undergraduate / TVET
Funding Fully Funded

About

The Albert Einstein German Academic Refugee Initiative (DAFI) enables refugee students to pursue undergraduate degrees and technical tertiary education in their country of asylum, promoting self-reliance, leadership, and local community development.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicants must hold official recognized refugee status or asylum-seeker status in their host country of asylum.
  • Candidates must be 28 years of age or younger at the start date of their higher education degree program.
  • Applicants must hold a certified secondary school leaving diploma or certificate with above-average academic grades.
  • Candidates must possess proof of admission, enrolment, or an active application to an accredited public or private university or TVET institute in their country of asylum.
  • Applicants must demonstrate proficiency in the primary language of instruction of their chosen degree program.
  • Only one member per immediate household family unit may hold an active DAFI scholarship concurrently.
  • Candidates who have been confirmed for third-country resettlement or who currently receive another tertiary scholarship are strictly ineligible.
  • Priority selection is given to young female applicants, students living with disabilities, and individuals from underrepresented refugee communities.

Essential Documentation Checklist

To submit a complete application to the local UNHCR country office or partner organization, candidates must prepare:

  • Proof of Legal Refugee Status: Copy of UNHCR Refugee Mandate Certificate, official Government Refugee ID card, or Asylum Seeker Attestation.
  • Certified Academic Credentials: Certified copy of secondary school completion certificate/result slip alongside official academic transcripts.
  • University Admission Document: Official letter of admission or proof of acceptance into an eligible undergraduate degree or diploma program.
  • Motivation Essay (Max 500 Words): Written personal statement outlining educational motivation, financial need, and commitment to community service.
  • Equivalence Certificate (Where Applicable): Qualification equivalence document issued by the Ministry of Education in the host country for foreign diplomas.
  • Passport Photographs: Two recent passport-sized photographs labeled with the applicant's full legal name.

What It Covers

  • 100% University Tuition Coverage: Full payment of academic tuition, registration, and laboratory fees directly to host educational institutions.
  • Monthly Living Stipend: Allowance covering daily personal costs, food, housing, and accommodation subsidies during study semesters.
  • Study Materials & Supplies Grant: Direct financial support for textbooks, stationery, software, and required educational supplies.
  • Transportation Allowance: Monthly grant covering local commuting costs between student residences and campus grounds.
  • Student Development & Clubs: Participation in DAFI student clubs, peer mentorship circles, academic monitoring, and local leadership development activities.

Application Deadline

Deadlines vary by individual country of asylum (typically opening annually between June and September via local UNHCR calls).

About

The University of Ottawa offers significant financial support and merit-based awards to international students pursuing undergraduate and graduate programs. Outstanding international candidates are recognized through renewable entrance scholarships, differential fee exemptions, and academic excellence awards.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicants must hold a secondary school completion diploma with a minimum overall admission average of 80.0% to qualify for basic entrance awards, or 90.0% and above for top-tier excellence funding.
  • There are no age restrictions or maximum age limits required to apply for or receive undergraduate entrance scholarships.
  • Candidates must apply for direct entry immediately following secondary school graduation without having accumulated prior post-secondary or university course credits.
  • Applicants must be newly admitted for the first time as full-time undergraduate students in a direct-entry faculty.
  • Candidates must hold international student status, possess a valid Canadian study permit, and be subject to paying full international tuition fees.
  • Students transferring from other post-secondary institutions, mature applicants with prior college experience, and non-degree students are ineligible for these awards.
  • Citizens of eligible African nations admitted to English-taught undergraduate degree programs automatically receive entrance funding valued between $60,000 and $100,000+ CAD spread across four years.
  • Students enrolling in a program taught in French or the French Immersion stream who complete at least three courses (9 units) per term in French qualify for domestic tuition rate exemptions.
  • Applicants must demonstrate language proficiency by achieving a minimum overall score of 6.5 in IELTS, 86 in TOEFL iBT, or 120–125 in the Duolingo English Test.
  • Award recipients must maintain continuous full-time registration and earn a Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of at least 7.0 out of 10.0 at the end of each academic year to retain multi-year funding.

Essential Documentation Checklist

To submit a complete application for admission and scholarship consideration, applicants must prepare:

  • Curriculum Vitae (CV): Detailing educational background, extracurricular leadership, community involvement, and achievements.
  • Academic Transcripts & Diplomas: Official high school transcripts and graduation certificates evaluated for Canadian equivalency.
  • Language Proficiency Scores: Official test results (IELTS, TOEFL iBT, or Duolingo English Test).
  • Personal Statement / Statement of Intent: Outlining academic and career goals (required for specific program applications and competitive bursaries).
  • Letters of Recommendation: Academic or professional references submitted via the application portal when required.

What It Covers

  • The **African Student Excellence Scheme** provides automatic tuition fee reductions ranging from $12,500 CAD to $18,000 CAD per term (up to $144,000 CAD across four years) depending on your faculty of study.
  • The **Differential Tuition Fee Exemption Scholarship** waives international rates for students enrolled in French or French Immersion tracks, reducing tuition down to Canadian domestic student rates.
  • The **International Admission Scholarship** awards direct financial grants valued at $1,000 CAD to $3,000 CAD per year based on top-tier admission averages.
  • The **President's and Chancellor's Scholarships** (for competitive applicants) offer prestigious renewable funding up to $30,000 CAD over four years ($7,500 CAD annually).
  • The **UOttawa University Health Insurance Plan (UHIP)** provides mandatory basic medical and hospital insurance coverage for international students throughout the duration of study.
  • The **UOPASS Campus Transit Pass** is included in incidental campus fees to cover unlimited local bus and O-Train light rail transit within Ottawa.

Application Deadline

Deadlines vary between February and April for Fall intake (September start), and around October for Winter intake, depending on the faculty and degree level.
