DAAD Scholarship

Master's / PhD
Country Germany
Study Level Master's / PhD
Funding Fully Funded

About

The DAAD (German Academic Exchange Service) offers postgraduate scholarships to graduates and academics from developing and industrialised nations to pursue degree programs or research at state-recognized German universities.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Academic Degree: Must hold at least a Bachelor's degree (or equivalent) in a relevant discipline completed within the last 6 years.
  • Work Experience: Candidates for EPOS courses generally require at least 2 years of professional work experience after graduation.
  • Language Proficiency: English (IELTS/TOEFL) or German (TestDaF/DSH) depending on the medium of instruction for the program.
  • Target Group: Highly qualified graduates with strong academic track records committed to local development.

What It Covers

  • Monthly stipend (€934 for Master's, €1,300 for Doctoral candidates)
  • Health, accident, and personal liability insurance coverage
  • Travel allowance to and from Germany
  • Annual study/research allowance and language course subsidies

Application Deadline

Deadlines vary between August and November annually depending on the university course selected.

UNHCR DAFI Scholarship

Undergraduate
Country Various (Country of Asylum)
Study Level Undergraduate
Funding Fully Funded

About

The Albert Einstein German Academic Refugee Initiative (DAFI) enables refugee students to pursue undergraduate degrees in their host countries, promoting self-reliance and community empowerment.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Refugee Status: Must hold official UNHCR registration or official refugee status in the country of asylum.
  • Age Requirement: Typically 28 years or younger at the start of the study program.
  • Education: Valid secondary school leaving certificate/diploma with strong grades.
  • Family Rule: Only one member per household may hold a DAFI scholarship concurrently.
  • Priority Groups: Preference for young women, students with disabilities, and underrepresented refugee groups.

What It Covers

  • Full university tuition fees
  • Monthly living allowance and accommodation subsidies
  • Textbooks, study materials, and transportation support

Application Deadline

Varies by host country (typically opens between June and August annually).

About

The WUSC Student Refugee Program combines youth-to-youth resettlement with post-secondary education in Canada, providing permanent residency alongside education.

  • Marital Status: Must be single with no dependents/children.
  • Academic Record: Completion of secondary school (e.g., KCSE in Kenya with minimum grade B- to B+).
  • Language Proficiency: Strong oral and written English or French communication skills.

What It Covers

  • Canadian Permanent Residency sponsorship
  • First-year complete financial, housing, and social support by campus local committees
  • Full university tuition and ongoing academic support

Application Deadline

Applications typically open in February/March annually.

Study in Romania Scholarship

Country Romania
Study Level Bachelor's / Master's / PhD
Funding Full Tuition & Accommodation

About

Offered by the Romanian State through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MFA) to non-EU citizens wishing to complete higher education degrees in Romania.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Citizenship: Foreign citizens coming from all non-EU countries.
  • Academic Grade Point Average: Minimum average mark of 7 (out of 10) on the Romanian grading scale in high school/university.

What It Covers

  • Full tuition fees (including 1-year Romanian preparatory language year)
  • Free student dormitory accommodation subsidies
  • Monthly allowance stipend based on level of study

Application Deadline

Mid-March annually via the official MFA portal.
