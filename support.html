Protect yourself while applying

  • Verify opportunities through the official provider's website.
  • Never share passwords, verification codes, or unnecessary sensitive documents.
  • Be cautious of anyone requesting money in exchange for a guaranteed scholarship or admission.

Portico Kakuma shares information to help students explore opportunities. Always confirm requirements and deadlines with the relevant official provider.
