EducationUSA — Nairobi

U.S. University Application Advising

EducationUSA is a U.S. higher-education advising network that helps international students understand how to apply to accredited colleges and universities in the United States. Students can seek guidance before starting their applications; they do not need to have received university admission or a scholarship before contacting an adviser.

Who can seek advising?

Prospective undergraduate students can contact EducationUSA while they are still exploring their options. You do not have to wait until you have submitted an application, received admission, or won a scholarship.

Students in Kakuma can contact the Nairobi centre to enquire about accessing advising remotely. Confirm directly whether individual virtual appointments are currently available to students living in Kakuma.

What can EducationUSA help you with?
  1. Research your options: Explore U.S. universities, degree programmes, admission requirements, and application timelines.
  2. Understand funding: Research scholarships, financial aid, and ways to finance your education.
  3. Prepare applications: Understand required documents, testing, application procedures, and deadlines.
  4. Understand student visas: Learn about the relevant U.S. student visa process and required preparations.
  5. Prepare for departure: Learn about preparations for travelling to the United States and beginning university life.

These are the five broad stages of EducationUSA's advising framework. The guidance available at each stage depends on the services offered by the centre.

How do you book an advising appointment?

Contact EducationUSA Nairobi to enquire about individual university application advising. You can make this enquiry before starting an application or receiving a scholarship.

  1. Visit the official website.

    Open the EducationUSA Nairobi centre page to view its contact information and available advising details.

    EducationUSA Nairobi ↗
  2. Contact the advising centre.

    Use the centre's official contact option or telephone +254 20 363 6583 to ask how individual advising appointments are arranged.

    You can also try the official contact form:

    Contact EducationUSA Nairobi ↗
  3. Explain your situation and what you need.

    Explain that you are a prospective undergraduate student based in Kakuma, Kenya, and would like guidance on applying to U.S. universities. If travelling to Nairobi is difficult, ask whether individual virtual advising is available.

  4. Ask about registration requirements.

    Ask whether you need to complete a registration form, attend a group session, or obtain student membership before an individual appointment can be booked. Confirm these requirements directly with the centre.

  5. Confirm availability and any costs.

    Ask about available appointment dates and times, whether the session can take place online, and whether individual or follow-up advising has any fees or other conditions.

  6. Confirm your appointment details.

    If the centre offers you an appointment, follow its instructions to confirm it. Check the date, time, meeting platform, and any information or documents you should prepare.

  7. Prepare your questions.

    You can ask about choosing universities, admission requirements, scholarship and financial-aid options, application documents, deadlines, and the steps toward studying in the United States.

Example message to send

Dear EducationUSA Nairobi Team,

I am a prospective undergraduate student based in Kakuma, Kenya, and I would like to receive guidance on applying to universities in the United States. I am at the beginning of the process and would appreciate help understanding university options, admission requirements, and funding opportunities.

Could you please explain how I can book an individual advising appointment? Since travelling to Nairobi may be difficult, I would also like to know whether virtual appointments are available to students in Kakuma.

Please let me know whether registration or student membership is required, whether any fees apply, and whether follow-up appointments can be arranged as I progress through the application process.

Thank you for your time and assistance.

Kind regards,
A prospective undergraduate student

Important: Sending an enquiry is not the same as having a confirmed appointment. Wait for the centre's response and follow its instructions. Virtual access, appointment availability, registration requirements, and fees should be confirmed directly with EducationUSA Nairobi.

Can students in Kakuma receive virtual advising?

EducationUSA uses different advising formats across its network, including online resources and virtual activities. However, students should confirm whether the Nairobi centre currently accepts individual virtual advising requests from Kakuma.

Before booking, ask:

  • Are individual online advising appointments available to students living in Kakuma?
  • Can students receive guidance by email or participate in online group sessions?
  • What internet connection or technology is required to participate?
Are appointments free?

EducationUSA provides free general advising information and resources. However, students should confirm directly with the Nairobi centre whether individual appointments, follow-up sessions, or student membership have any fees or conditions.

University application fees, testing, document preparation, and visa-related expenses are separate from advising and may still apply. Check the relevant university and official authorities for current requirements.

Will one adviser guide you until departure?

EducationUSA provides guidance across the U.S. university application journey, but the Nairobi centre's public information does not guarantee that every student will receive one dedicated adviser throughout all five stages.

It also does not guarantee unlimited appointments, reserved follow-up sessions, or immediate availability whenever a student needs help.

Ask the centre whether follow-up appointments can be arranged in advance and whether ongoing individual guidance is available from university research through pre-departure preparation.

Important for students in Kakuma: EducationUSA focuses on U.S. higher education. It is an advising resource, not a scholarship provider and not a guaranteed long-term personal mentorship programme. Confirm virtual access, appointment availability, fees, and follow-up arrangements directly with the Nairobi centre.
