Frequently Asked Questions

Can I apply if I am still completing high school or university?
Yes. Some scholarships are open to final-year students or applicants expecting to graduate before the programme begins. Check the eligibility requirements carefully.
How can I improve my chances of winning a scholarship?
Apply early, follow all instructions, submit complete documents, write strong essays, and tailor every application to the scholarship's requirements.
What if I don't have perfect grades?
Don't be discouraged. Many scholarships consider leadership, community service, work experience, financial need, and personal achievements in addition to academic performance.
What documents are commonly required?
Common documents include academic transcripts, certificates, a CV, a motivation letter, recommendation letters, a passport or national ID, and proof of English proficiency if required.
Can I apply for more than one scholarship?
Yes. You are encouraged to apply for multiple scholarships as long as you meet the eligibility requirements for each one.
Do I need to pay to apply for a scholarship?
Most scholarship applications are free. Be cautious of anyone asking you to pay an application fee for a scholarship, as this could be a scam. If at all there is an application fee and you are not un a position to pay, you may opt for "fee waiver" to help cover the cost.
Are all scholarships fully funded?
No. Some scholarships cover all expenses, while others only pay for tuition, accommodation, travel, or provide a partial award. Always read the scholarship benefits carefully.
How to apply without a passport.
A passport is not always required to start an application. Some scholarships accept alternative documents such as a national ID or refugee ID. Always check the specific scholarship's requirements before applying. However, a passport or refugee travel documents will be required in later stages so it is advisable to prepare them in advance.
How to apply when you cannot afford application fee.
Some scholarships offer fee waivers or have no application fee. Check the official application page or contact the institution to ask if you qualify for a waiver or exemption. Never pay unofficial agents.
What documents are usually required?
Common documents include academic transcripts, CV/resume, motivation letter, recommendation letters, language test scores, and proof of identity.
Can I apply for multiple scholarships?
Yes, you can apply for multiple scholarships simultaneously. In fact, it is recommended to increase your chances of receiving funding.
Do I need IELTS to apply?
Many scholarships require English proficiency proof. IELTS, TOEFL, and Duolingo are commonly accepted. Some scholarships may waive this requirement.
How are scholarship recipients selected?
Selection is based on academic merit, leadership potential, community involvement, quality of application materials, and sometimes interviews.
How do I prepare for a scholarship test?
Start by finding out what subjects and topics the scholarship test covers. Review your school notes and textbooks, practise past papers and sample questions, and work on your time management. Focus on areas where you need more practice and take timed practice tests to become comfortable answering questions under pressure.
Which subjects are there for the scholarship exam?
Exams typically test core academic subjects, general aptitude, and reasoning skills, depending on the specific grade level and board.
What should I write for why I deserve this scholarship?
Start with a clear hook, show the impact the scholarship would have on your education and future, and connect it to your long-term goals. Focus on your growth, experiences, and how you have overcome challenges. Finally, edit and polish your response to make sure it is clear, personal, and well written.
