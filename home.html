Why Portico Kakuma

A portico is an entrance or sheltered space leading into a building. We chose the name because we see this platform as an entrance to new possibilities. Portico Kakuma is here to help refugee students and students from underserved communities discover scholarships and educational opportunities, understand what they need to apply, and find practical resources to prepare for their next step. You don't have to figure everything out alone. Start where you are, explore what's available, and take your next step.

Why Scholarships Matter

For many students in Kakuma, the dream of higher education is there, but the financial means are not. Scholarships can make that next step possible, giving students a chance to continue their education and pursue the future they have worked toward. We want you to know that financial hardship does not have to be the end of your journey. There are opportunities out there, and sometimes finding them is the first step. Keep looking. Keep preparing. Keep applying. Your circumstances today do not have to define where you end up.

Tips for a Successful Application

  1. Take time to do your research on your course carefully and make sure it genuinely matches your interests, strengths, and long-term goals.
  2. Check the entry requirements for your country, look closely at any English language requirements, and give yourself enough time to prepare supporting documents.
  3. Tailor every application to the specific scholarship or university by connecting your goals, experiences, and strengths to what the opportunity is looking for.
  4. Proofread your application carefully before submitting it, checking for spelling, grammar, missing information, and mistakes in names, dates, or contact details.
  5. If you are applying through a program or government scheme, make sure your application is complete and accurate, and use your personal statement to show clear motivation, relevant experience, and what you hope to achieve at university.
  6. Once you have applied, keep track of deadlines and check student portals and emails regularly so you know what happens next and how to prepare if you receive an offer.
  7. Use a professional email address when applying or communicating with universities and scholarship providers, preferably one that clearly identifies you by name.
  8. Keep digital copies of your important documents, including certificates, transcripts, identification, recommendation letters, and submitted applications, in a safe and organized place.
  9. Be honest and accurate throughout your application, and make sure that all the information you provide reflects your real experiences, achievements, and qualifications.
  10. Choose your referees early and give them enough time to prepare a strong recommendation from someone who knows your work, character, and achievements well.
  11. Prepare in advance for scholarship interviews by reviewing your application, researching the opportunity, and practicing how you will explain your goals, experiences, and future plans.
  12. Stay positive and keep trying even if you face rejection.
