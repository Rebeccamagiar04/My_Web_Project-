DOCUMENT GUIDE

Explore the documents you may need when preparing scholarship and university applications.

Choose a category

Swipe through the categories and tap one to view its documents.

01

Identification & Status

Documents you may need

  • Passport
  • Refugee identification document
  • UNHCR documentation
  • National identification card
  • Birth certificate
  • Proof of nationality
  • Proof of legal residence
  • Other official identification documents, where applicable

What is it?

Documents that may be used to confirm your identity, nationality, or legal status.

What may you need them for?

  • University applications
  • Scholarship applications
  • Visa applications

💡 Tips

  • Don't have one of these documents? Check whether the institution accepts alternative documentation.
  • Check your name carefully and make sure it is consistent across your documents.

02

Academic Documents

Documents you may need

  • Secondary school certificate
  • Academic results slip
  • Academic transcript
  • School leaving certificate
  • Diploma or degree certificate
  • Grading scale
  • Other academic records, where applicable

What is it?

Documents that show your educational background, academic results, qualifications, and previous study.

What may you need them for?

  • University admission
  • Scholarship applications
  • Academic verification

💡 Tips

  • Keep clear digital copies of your academic documents.
  • Check whether documents need to be translated or certified.

03

Language Proficiency

Documents you may need

  • IELTS results
  • TOEFL results
  • Duolingo English Test results
  • Other accepted language-test results
  • Proof of English-medium education, where accepted

What is it?

Documents that show your ability to study or communicate in the language required by a university or scholarship.

What may you need them for?

  • University admission
  • Scholarship applications
  • Meeting language requirements

💡 Tips

  • Check which language tests your institution accepts.
  • Check the required score and validity period before testing.

04

Essays & Statements

Documents you may need

  • Motivation letter
  • Personal statement
  • Statement of purpose
  • Study plan
  • Scholarship essays
  • Other written responses requested by the provider

What is it?

Written documents that help an institution understand your goals, experiences, motivation, and plans for the future.

What may you need them for?

  • Scholarship applications
  • University applications
  • Explaining your academic or career goals

💡 Tips

  • Answer the exact question being asked.
  • Use specific experiences rather than general statements.

05

Recommendations

Documents you may need

  • Recommendation letters
  • Academic references
  • Teacher or counsellor references
  • Employer references, where applicable
  • Referee contact information

What is it?

Letters or references from people who can speak about your academic background, character, skills, or experience.

What may you need them for?

  • Scholarship applications
  • University admission
  • Supporting your experience and achievements

💡 Tips

  • Ask your referee early rather than waiting until the deadline.
  • Choose someone who knows your work or experience well.

06

Experience & Portfolio

Documents you may need

  • CV or résumé
  • Work experience records
  • Volunteer certificates
  • Leadership certificates
  • Portfolio
  • Awards and achievements
  • Proof of extracurricular activities
  • Research work or publications, where applicable

What is it?

Documents and materials that show what you have done outside your formal academic records.

What may you need them for?

  • Scholarship applications
  • University applications
  • Showing skills, leadership, or experience

💡 Tips

  • Keep certificates and evidence of important activities in one place.
  • Focus on experiences that are relevant to the application.

07

Health & Travel

Documents you may need

  • Medical examination form or certificate
  • Vaccination records
  • Health insurance documents
  • Visa documents
  • Admission or offer letter
  • Proof of funds, where required
  • Accommodation confirmation
  • Travel insurance, where required
  • Visa photographs

What is it?

Documents that may be required when preparing for travel, visa processing, health checks, or relocation for study.

What may you need them for?

  • Visa applications
  • Travel preparation
  • University or scholarship requirements

💡 Tips

  • Check the current requirements for your destination.
  • Start preparing travel documents early if you are admitted.
