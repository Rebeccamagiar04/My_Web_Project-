Get to know us

Portico Kakuma is a small, independent project built to help you pursue your educational dreams with confidence. We’re here to guide and support you along the way, but scholarships are never guaranteed. Our services are completely free, so please beware of anyone asking for money on our behalf. You’re not alone on this journey! Best of luck as you apply.

OUR PURPOSE

Our Mission

We believe where a student is born should never determine how far they can go.

Our mission is to bridge the information gap preventing refugee and underserved students from accessing higher education opportunities.

Through this platform, we simplify scholarship discovery, application guidance, university information and deadlines in one accessible place.

We also support the UNHCR 15by30 strategy by helping more students discover and apply for higher education.

Our Vision

We envision a future where every refugee student can easily access quality higher education opportunities, regardless of their background.

We aspire to become Africa's trusted scholarship platform connecting learners with universities, scholarships, mentorship and educational resources.

Why I Created Portico Kakuma

For refugee youth, dreaming of higher education often feels like reaching for something just out of reach. It isn’t a lack of ambition, or effort—it is the overwhelming barrier of invisible walls.

​Because opportunities are so limited and hard to find, many of us have grown accustomed to settling for less. We begin to make peace with whatever small options come our way, slowly accepting the idea that we are somehow meant to receive less than others. I strongly believe that no one should ever be forced to get used to being "less than." Your background or refugee status should never dictate the ceiling of your potential or lower the standard of what you deserve.

​For a long time, most of us in the community only knew about a few, well-trodden scholarship pathways. Because it was the only option widely known, thousands of brilliant, qualified applicants competed for a handful of spots every single cycle. For three straight years, I put my heart into applying through that same familiar route. And three times in a row, I received rejections. It left me feeling stuck, wondering if it was bad luck, or if my dreams were simply too big for my circumstances.

​It wasn't until later that I realized the real problem: we are limited not by our potential, but by a lack of information and access.

​There are many other global scholarships, university programs, and educational pathways out there worth pursuing. While some programs may offer different levels of support, being in a university anywhere means your dream is actively within reach. Yet most refugees never learn these opportunities exist. And for those who do, application processes can be so complex and rigid that many give up before even finishing.

​I built the resource I wish I had three years ago. My goal is to diversify your options and increase your chances of getting into a university
