OUR PURPOSE

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

We believe where a student is born should never determine how far they can go.

Our mission is to bridge the information gap preventing refugee and underserved students from accessing higher education opportunities.

Through this platform, we simplify scholarship discovery, application guidance, university information and deadlines in one accessible place.

We also support the UNHCR 15by30 strategy by helping more students discover and apply for higher education.

Our Vision

We envision a future where every refugee student can easily access quality higher education opportunities, regardless of their background.

We aspire to become Africa's trusted scholarship platform connecting learners with universities, scholarships, mentorship and educational resources.

OUR STORY

Why I Created Portico Kakuma

Hi, my name is Atem Rebecca the founder of Portico Kakuma. After finishing high school, I was determined to continue my education and like many students in Kakuma, I knew about some of the common scholarship opportunities available to refugees.

I applied for one of them and placed most of my hopes in that opportunity. I believed that if I worked hard and made it through the process, university would finally be within reach.

But I wasn't selected.

I decided to try again the following year. This time, I was even more hopeful. I thought that surely this would be my opportunity. But once again, I received a rejection.

That was when I started asking myself: Why did I only apply for one opportunity?

I realized that I had not known enough about the other scholarships and pathways available to students like me. I had become conditioned to think that the opportunity I knew about was the best I could get.

That experience changed the way I looked at scholarship opportunities. I realized that students shouldn't have to put all their hopes into one application and only discover other possibilities after receiving a rejection.

That's why I created Portico Kakuma — to make scholarship opportunities easier to discover and to encourage students to explore more than one path toward higher education.

Being a refugee should not condition us to believe that we have to settle for less. We can dream beyond our circumstances, apply alongside our classmates, and pursue opportunities that can take us where we want to go.

I created this platform so that another student doesn't have to learn that lesson the hard way.
