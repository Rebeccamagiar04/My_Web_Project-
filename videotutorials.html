How to fill out Common App

In this video, you're guided through the entire process of applying to college using the Common App for the 2026–2027 application cycle. You start by learning how to set up a first-year student account on commonapp.org and fill out the core application section, which gets sent to all your selected schools. You're shown key features like checking your eligibility for a fee waiver to eliminate application costs, selecting an essay topic from seven available prompts, and using the College Search tool to explore and add schools to your personal list. Next, you learn how to use your Dashboard to track application progress, upcoming deadlines, and specific school requirements. In the "My Colleges" section, you're shown how to answer school-specific questions, complete the FERPA release authorization so your high school can send official records, and invite recommenders like teachers and counselors. Finally, you walk through reviewing your completed application, submitting fees or waivers, signing the final affirmations, and submitting your application to reach the final confetti celebration.
