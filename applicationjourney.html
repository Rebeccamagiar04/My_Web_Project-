Application Journey

A step-by-step guide to help you navigate the scholarship application process from finding an opportunity to arriving at university.

STEP 1 OF 12

Identify the Right Opportunity

The first step is finding a scholarship or programme that matches your goals, background and eligibility. Take time to research opportunities carefully before starting an application.

What to do

  • Define your academic and career goals.
  • Search for scholarships that match your field of study and education level.
  • Check whether you meet the eligibility requirements.
  • Compare different opportunities before deciding where to apply.

What to look for

  • Nationality or refugee eligibility.
  • Academic requirements.
  • Course and study level.
  • Language requirements.
  • Funding type.
  • Application deadline.
  • Study location.

Watch out for

  • Applying for opportunities you are not eligible for.
  • Ignoring important requirements.
  • Missing application deadlines.
  • Scholarship scams and fake opportunities.
Tip: Create a simple list or spreadsheet to track scholarships, deadlines and requirements.
STEP 2 OF 12

Prepare Before the Application Opens

Preparing early can make the application process much easier. Gather your documents and information before the application portal opens.

Prepare your documents

  • Academic certificates and transcripts.
  • Identification documents.
  • Passport where required.
  • CV or résumé.
  • Recommendation letters.
  • English-language test results, if required.

Prepare your information

  • Academic history.
  • Work or volunteer experience.
  • Extracurricular activities.
  • Achievements and awards.
  • Career and study goals.

Watch out for

  • Waiting until the deadline to request documents.
  • Using expired or incorrect documents.
  • Saving important files in only one location.
Tip: Create one scholarship folder on your phone or computer where you can keep all your application documents.
STEP 3 OF 12

The Application Opens

Once the application opens, carefully read the official instructions before you begin filling in the application.

What to do

  • Create an account on the official application portal.
  • Read all application instructions.
  • Check the required documents again.
  • Note the application deadline and time zone.
  • Save your login details securely.

Before you start

  • Make sure you have a stable internet connection.
  • Have your documents ready.
  • Understand the questions you will need to answer.
  • Give yourself enough time to complete the application.

Watch out for

  • Using unofficial application websites.
  • Ignoring application instructions.
  • Leaving the application until the last day.
STEP 4 OF 12

Write & Review Your Application

This is your opportunity to clearly present your experiences, goals, achievements and reasons for applying.

What to do

  • Answer every question carefully.
  • Complete essays and motivation questions.
  • Highlight relevant experiences and achievements.
  • Explain your academic and career goals.
  • Make sure your answers address the actual questions.

Review your application

  • Check spelling and grammar.
  • Check word or character limits.
  • Make sure your information is consistent.
  • Ask someone you trust to review your work where possible.

Need help?

For detailed guidance on motivation letters, essays and other application writing, visit our Video Tutorials and writing resources.

STEP 5 OF 12

Submit Your Application

Before clicking submit, take a final moment to make sure your application is complete and accurate.

Final checklist

  • All required questions are answered.
  • All required documents are uploaded.
  • Files are in the correct format.
  • Files meet the required size limits.
  • Your contact information is correct.

After submitting

  • Save your confirmation message.
  • Download your application if possible.
  • Save your application reference number.
  • Keep a copy of important emails.
Tip: Do not wait until the final hours to submit your application.
STEP 6 OF 12

After Submission

Submitting your application does not always mean the process is finished. There may be several weeks or months of waiting before you receive a decision.

What to do

  • Monitor your email regularly.
  • Check the application portal when necessary.
  • Respond quickly if additional information is requested.
  • Keep your contact details updated.

While you wait

  • Continue developing your skills.
  • Look for other suitable opportunities.
  • Prepare for possible interviews.
  • Keep your important documents updated.
STEP 7 OF 12

Shortlisting

If your application passes the initial review, you may be shortlisted for the next stage of the selection process.

What shortlisted means

Being shortlisted generally means your application has progressed to another stage. It does not always mean that you have received the scholarship yet.

What may happen next

  • You may receive an interview invitation.
  • Additional documents may be requested.
  • You may be asked to complete another assessment.
  • You may receive instructions for the next stage.

What to do

  • Read the invitation carefully.
  • Respond before the stated deadline.
  • Begin preparing for the next stage immediately.
STEP 8 OF 12

The Interview Stage

Some scholarships invite shortlisted applicants to an interview. This is an opportunity to discuss your goals, experiences and suitability for the programme.

Prepare

  • Research the scholarship and university.
  • Review your application.
  • Think about your goals and experiences.
  • Prepare for common interview questions.

If the interview is online

  • Test your internet connection.
  • Check your camera and microphone.
  • Choose a quiet place.
  • Join early.

Remember

Be honest, confident and yourself. You do not need to have a perfect answer to every question.

STEP 9 OF 12

Selection & Final Decisions

After interviews and final assessments, the scholarship provider will make its final decisions.

Possible outcomes

  • Selected.
  • Waitlisted.
  • Unsuccessful.
  • Conditional offer.

If you are selected

  • Read the offer carefully.
  • Check the acceptance deadline.
  • Understand the scholarship conditions.
  • Follow the instructions provided.

If you are not selected

An unsuccessful application does not mean you cannot succeed elsewhere. Review what you can improve and continue looking for other opportunities.

STEP 10 OF 12

After Receiving the Scholarship

Congratulations! Receiving a scholarship is exciting, but there may still be important steps to complete before your place is fully confirmed.

What you may need to do

  • Accept the scholarship or university offer.
  • Submit any final documents requested.
  • Respond to emails from the university or scholarship provider.
  • Complete admission or scholarship registration.
  • Fulfil any conditions attached to your offer.
  • Keep copies of confirmation emails and documents.

Stay organised

  • Keep track of important deadlines.
  • Save official correspondence.
  • Follow every instruction provided.
  • Ask the scholarship provider if something is unclear.
Important: Receiving a scholarship does not always mean every requirement has been completed. Check your offer carefully.
STEP 11 OF 12

Visa & Travel Preparations

If you are studying in another country, you may need to complete visa and travel preparations before you can begin your studies.

Visa preparation

  • Check the visa requirements for your destination.
  • Gather the required documents.
  • Complete the visa application.
  • Attend biometrics or an interview if required.
  • Track your visa application.

Travel preparation

  • Follow instructions from your university or scholarship provider.
  • Confirm when you are expected to arrive.
  • Arrange your travel when appropriate.
  • Keep important documents accessible while travelling.

Important

Visa and travel requirements vary by country, university and scholarship programme. Always follow official instructions.

STEP 12 OF 12

Accommodation & University Arrival

You have made it to the final stage! Now it is time to prepare for your move and your first days at university.

Accommodation

  • Check whether accommodation is provided.
  • Apply for university accommodation if necessary.
  • Understand your accommodation arrangements.
  • Know where you will stay when you arrive.

Prepare for arrival

  • Pack important documents.
  • Keep your passport and travel documents secure.
  • Prepare essential items for your first few days.
  • Learn about your university and surroundings.

At the university

  • Complete university registration.
  • Get your student identification where applicable.
  • Attend orientation.
  • Learn about scholarship requirements.
  • Know who to contact when you need help.
You've made it! Your scholarship journey doesn't end here — this is the beginning of your university experience.
